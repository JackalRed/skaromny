How I think about products

A link is not a track ID.

The hard part is not generating a list. It is earning trust in every match. That means thinking about the signal, candidate generation, version differences, and human judgment.

ACCURACY

When confidence is low, show alternatives instead of confidently choosing the wrong song.

COST

Use cheaper, reliable signals first; escalate to more expensive models only where needed.

UX

Keep the listener in control wherever automation can break trust.
