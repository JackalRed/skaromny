03 / PROOF OF WORK
needle✳

A product born from
an everyday frustration.

Needle turns a link from a Reel or YouTube video into candidate music matches you can review and collect in a Spotify playlist. The challenge is preserving the listener's intent from discovery through playlist creation.

THE ACTUAL PRODUCT JOURNEY

    Capture a moment.

  2. 02

    Review the music.

  3. 03

    Keep it.

04 / PRODUCT JUDGEMENT

The hard part was
earning trust.

A link is not a track ID. Recognising a melody still leaves the question of which version belongs in the playlist. Shipping the first result is easy; earning trust is harder.

ACCURACY

Low-confidence matches should offer alternatives and leave the choice with the listener.

COST

Start with inexpensive signals; escalate only when uncertainty demands it.

UX

Keep the listener's judgement where a wrong match would break the experience.
