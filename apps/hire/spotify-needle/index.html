01 / THE REASON

The best discoveries
don't always happen
inside a music app.

Sometimes it's a Reel, a YouTube mix or a clip from a friend. I kept finding music I loved and losing it on the way to my library. Needle started with that very ordinary frustration.

02 / THE PRODUCT JUDGEMENT

The important part
wasn't recognition.

Anyone can demo an AI result. I cared about what happens when the result is uncertain, slow or almost right.

01 / TRUST

Let the listener choose.

When the system isn't sure which track or version fits, show candidates clearly.

02 / COST

Use intelligence deliberately.

Start with cheap, reliable signals. Escalate to expensive inference when needed.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Keep the flow human.

The aim is a playlist worth saving, with control over the final selections.

REALITY CHECK

In the filmed example, 22 tracks were selected. It's a demonstration of a working flow, not a production performance claim. Waiting periods were edited out.
