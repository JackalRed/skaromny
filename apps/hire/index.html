01 / CONSUMER GROWTHGrowing an app already used by millions.MegaFon · 16M MAU+
CHALLENGE

At 16M monthly active users, growth required more than shipping another feature. We needed to give more customers reasons to open the app and make the wider ecosystem relevant to their everyday behaviour.

MY DECISIONS

Expanded access to a new segment, supported an in-app streaming integration and reorganised delivery around faster execution and measurable engagement.

OUTCOMES

+1M MAU. Monthly visit frequency increased 25% following the streaming integration; average production release lead time fell by 2 days. ARPU rose 15% during my tenure.

02 / B2B COMMERCEMake buying digital services dramatically easier.MegaFon B2B · 2.5× paid orders+
CHALLENGE

Customers had to navigate disconnected services and purchasing journeys, creating unnecessary friction between intent and a completed order.

MY DECISIONS

Helped unify fragmented product pages and buying journeys into a single commerce destination, introduced enterprise SSO and redesigned delivery routines to ship improvements faster.

OUTCOMES

Paid product orders increased 2.5×; feature delivery became 50% faster. A clearer buying journey connected platform simplification to a commercial result.

03 / VODAFONE · KNOWLEDGE AGENTSFrom isolated AI demos to an agent portfolio with a value case.Projected benefit potential: ~7M · not realised savings+
THE PROBLEM

Disconnected AI experiments are difficult to prioritise, govern and turn into workflows that teams actually use. Without shared evaluation, a promising agent can remain a demo.

MY APPROACH

Organised knowledge agents as an intentional portfolio: demand intake, trusted knowledge, governance, quality assessment and adoption. Connected product choices to the expected operational benefit of each use case.

VALUE CASE

An internal portfolio assessment identified approximately 7M in potential benefits across knowledge-agent opportunities. This is a forecast/portfolio estimate, not a realised financial return or a claim of completed rollout.

04 / VODAFONE · SMART CAPEXMaking tower investment decisions more intelligent.Telecom infrastructure · investment prioritisation+
THE PROBLEM

Telecom tower and site investment choices require a consistent view of value, costs and alternatives. Expert judgement matters, but ad-hoc comparisons make portfolio trade-offs harder.

MY APPROACH

Developed the Smart CAPEX use case for structured evaluation of tower/site investment options, referencing BCG benchmarking material to inform comparisons and support capital-allocation discussions.

WHAT THIS ENABLES

A more explainable basis for comparing scenarios and surfacing investment priorities. Presented as a decision-support use case; no unverified deployed ROI or endorsement by BCG is claimed.
