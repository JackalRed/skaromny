04 / VODAFONE · SMART CAPEXWhere should a network invest its next euro?Smart CAPEX · forecast → capacity → financial return+
THE INVESTMENT PROBLEM

Network teams must decide which towers and sites to upgrade before congestion damages customer experience and revenue. Technical urgency alone is not enough: every site competes for scarce capital.

THE PRODUCT

Shaped Smart CAPEX around 12-month traffic forecasts, predicted congestion, potential lost revenue and the cost of technical alternatives. Ranked site investments by ROI, ROCE and payback — making network performance a capital-allocation decision.

THE ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY

BCG's published telecom research estimates 4% lower average network spending from site-specific forecasting and 15% from value-based investment prioritisation. These are external industry benchmarks ↗ informing the opportunity, not claimed Vodafone savings.
