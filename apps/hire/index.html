01 / CONSUMER GROWTHGrowing an app already used by millions.MegaFon · 16M MAU+
CHALLENGE

At 16M monthly active users, growth required more than shipping another feature. We needed to give more customers reasons to open the app and make the wider ecosystem relevant to their everyday behaviour.

MY DECISIONS

Expanded access to a new segment, supported an in-app streaming integration and reorganised delivery around faster execution and measurable engagement.

OUTCOMES

+1M MAU. Monthly visit frequency increased 25% following the streaming integration; average production release lead time fell by 2 days. ARPU rose 15% during my tenure.

02 / B2B COMMERCEMake buying digital services dramatically easier.MegaFon B2B · 2.5× paid orders+
CHALLENGE

Customers had to navigate disconnected services and purchasing journeys, creating unnecessary friction between intent and a completed order.

MY DECISIONS

Helped unify fragmented product pages and buying journeys into a single commerce destination, introduced enterprise SSO and redesigned delivery routines to ship improvements faster.

OUTCOMES

Paid product orders increased 2.5×; feature delivery became 50% faster. A clearer buying journey connected platform simplification to a commercial result.

03 / ENTERPRISE AITurn scattered AI initiatives into a product capability.Vodafone Group · AI Workforce Augmentation+
CHALLENGE

AI prototypes are easy to start. Making them dependable, usable and governable in complex network operations requires product ownership, trusted knowledge and a way to measure value.

MY DECISIONS

Established an AI product portfolio framework connecting intake, knowledge management, agent lifecycle governance and evaluation. Put quality, adoption and task outcomes into the product conversation.

PROGRESS

Created a shared approach for prioritising and evaluating AI-enabled workflows across teams. This is an operating-model and portfolio case — **not a claimed financial return or completed enterprise-wide rollout**.
